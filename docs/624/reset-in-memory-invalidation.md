## Scope and ordering

- Reset must receive an explicit success/failure result from in-memory invalidation. Poisoned/unavailable registry state must not silently produce a completion acknowledgment.
- Ordinary submissions are invalidated only for the exact database identity held by a live database-admission pause. The pause must verify its own token and require all owners released before registry mutation. Wrong-database, still-active and unavailable pauses fail without pretending to drain workers.
- With admission still paused, lock the submission registry, then lock all matching submission states in the same registry-before-state order as begin/retirement. Validate no active owners or workers before applying invalidation. Do not delete raw input, accepted payload, permission fence, committed receipt, attempt history, failure, cleanup or unknown-effect facts. A dedicated permission-invalidated flag permanently blocks retry of the old submission, including after pause release. A fresh submission created after release may proceed normally. Unrelated databases are unchanged.
- Replay-session invalidation gains an acknowledged operation. Successful invalidation removes idle sessions and current generations so an outstanding old lease cannot reseal/repopulate them; it does not clear active-owner evidence or claim that an active runtime stopped. New leases after invalidation may work normally. Generation allocation must fail closed on exhaustion instead of reusing a saturated generation.
- Invalidated ordinary retry displays a specific revoked-authorization reason without claiming the entire reset completed. Known failed sends have no Retry or Check status action and direct the user to review the retained message before a fresh send. Unknown send results retain their uncertainty warning and Check status action, but neither a previous nor a late status response may hide the revoked-authorization reason or suggest that polling can restore old authority. Existing copy/edit access to raw content remains available.
- Existing non-reset best-effort invalidation callers may retain their behavior. Reset must use the acknowledged operation and propagate failure. These APIs alone do not pause scheduler/authorization changes, stop native work, rotate the durable epoch or produce a reset/downgrade receipt; the future coordinator must keep the database pause across all those steps.

## Development validation

Use isolated registries and in-memory fixture databases; no real config, credential, shell, process or network access. Exercise actual retry decisions and replay begin/seal behavior, not source text or synthetic boolean substitutes:

1. A genuinely retryable ordinary submission becomes non-retryable after acknowledged invalidation; raw/accepted/attempt/commit/cleanup evidence remains available, repeated invalidation is safe, another database and a fresh post-pause submission remain usable.
2. Wrong database and active owners are refused before invalidation. A poisoned submission state produces an error without partial mutation of other locked states; unknown-effect facts are preserved.
3. A valid idle replay session is reusable before invalidation and unavailable afterward. An old active lease cannot reseal after invalidation; a newly admitted lease has a positive seal/reuse control. Poison and generation exhaustion fail closed.
4.
